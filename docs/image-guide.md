## Compression Guidance

- Prefer AVIF or WebP for photographic images.
- Keep hero images under 450 KB when practical.
- Keep product images under 250 KB each where possible.
- Preserve enough shadow and bottle detail for dark backgrounds.
- Do not embed important text inside images.
